# LegalLens AI – Research Benchmarks & Evaluation
**Directory:** `/benchmarks`  
**Purpose:** Independent, reproducible evaluation scripts, dataloaders, and metric harnesses.

## Included Benchmark Suites (Refer to RESEARCH_EVALUATION.md)
1. **ILDC (Indian Legal Documents Corpus):** Supreme Court case outcome prediction and judicial ratio ranking.
2. **InLegalNER:** Indian legal Named Entity Recognition (14 entity classes).
3. **LegalEval (SemEval-2023 Task 6):** Rhetorical role classification, court judgment summarization.
4. **ContractNLI:** Contractual premise-hypothesis entailment and contradiction benchmark.
5. **CUAD (Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset):** Clause boundary extraction.
6. **LegalLens-Risk-IN (Proposed Dataset Protocol):** 1,500 expert-annotated Indian contract clauses across 7 risk categories.
